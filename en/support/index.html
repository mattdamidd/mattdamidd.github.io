Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to mattdamidd @ 2rs.de.

To report a bug, please include:

  • what you did and what you expected to happen
  • what happened instead
  • your device (for example iPhone 16) and the iOS version
  • the language of the app, English or German

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open the rules from the lobby. They explain the cards, the Midd, trump and scoring.

Multiplayer does not find the other devices

  • All devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Multiplayer must be turned on in the settings of every device.
  • iOS must allow the app to use the local network: open Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, and turn on Matt da Midd.
  • Some guest or public Wi-Fi networks stop devices from seeing each other. Try another network.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. The solo match needs no network. Multiplayer needs a local Wi-Fi network, but no internet connection.

What data does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy.

What does a match consist of?

A match is one or more games with the same players. A game is played over several rounds until one team has struck out all its score lines. After a game you can continue with the same players or end the match.
