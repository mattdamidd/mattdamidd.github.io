The app

Matt da Midd does not collect, store or send any personal data to the provider or to third parties. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Stored on your device only. The app saves your player name, its settings and the state of your matches on your device. This data never leaves your device, except as described below for multiplayer. You can remove it by deleting the app.

Multiplayer on the local network. If you turn multiplayer on, the app finds and talks to other devices running the app on the same local network (Wi-Fi). iOS asks you for the permission “Local Network” the first time. The devices exchange your player name and the moves of the match directly with each other. No server is involved, and nothing is sent over the internet. Other players in your match can see your player name. You can turn multiplayer off at any time in the settings, and the app then does not use the network at all.

No other data. The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not read your device identifiers.
