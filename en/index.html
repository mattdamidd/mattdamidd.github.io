Matt da Midd

A trick-taking card game for four

Matt da Midd is a card game from the German-Luxembourg border region. Two teams of two play for the Midd, the Queen of Spades. Play alone against computer players, or with friends on the same Wi-Fi.

Download

Solo or together

Play a match against three computer players, or invite friends nearby and play on your own devices.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app is free. It collects no data and needs no sign-up.

Works offline

The solo match needs no network. Multiplayer talks directly between the devices on your local network.

English and German

The app speaks both languages, including VoiceOver.
